<section id="home">
    <h2>About This Project</h2>
    <p>
        Environmental awareness means understanding the importance of protecting
        our natural resources and maintaining ecological balance.
        Rapid industrialization, deforestation, and urbanization have increased
        environmental problems worldwide. This project focuses on major
        environmental challenges and their practical solutions.
    </p>
</section>

<article id="climate">
    <h2>1. Climate Change</h2>
    <p>
        Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns.
        It is mainly caused by greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide (CO₂) and methane.
        Burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial activities increase global warming.
    </p>
    <p>
        Effects include rising sea levels, melting glaciers, extreme weather events,
        and disruption of ecosystems.
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>Switch to renewable energy sources like solar and wind</li>
        <li>Reduce use of fossil fuels</li>
        <li>Promote energy-efficient appliances</li>
        <li>Encourage public transportation and cycling</li>
    </ul>
    <a href="https://www.climate.gov/" target="_blank">
        <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1502082553048-f009c37129b9" height="200" alt="Climate Change">
    </a>
</article>

<article id="pollution">
    <h2>2. Pollution Control</h2>
    <p>
        Pollution occurs when harmful substances contaminate the environment.
        Air pollution is caused by vehicle emissions and industries.
        Water pollution happens due to sewage and chemical waste.
        Soil pollution is caused by pesticides and plastic waste.
    </p>
    <ol>
        <li>Reduce plastic consumption</li>
        <li>Recycle and reuse materials</li>
        <li>Control industrial waste disposal</li>
        <li>Adopt eco-friendly products</li>
    </ol>
    <a href="https://www.populationfoundation.in/" target="_blank">
        <img src="https://blog.ipleaders.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/pollution-bad_fbd596fd1a506e77.jpg" height="200" alt="Pollution Control">
    </a>
</article>

<article id="wildlife">
    <h2>3. Wildlife Protection</h2>
    <p>
        Wildlife plays a crucial role in maintaining biodiversity and ecological balance.
        Deforestation and illegal poaching threaten many species with extinction.
        Protecting wildlife ensures stable ecosystems and natural food chains.
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>Prevent illegal hunting and poaching</li>
        <li>Establish wildlife sanctuaries</li>
        <li>Promote forest conservation</li>
        <li>Create awareness about endangered species</li>
    </ul>
    <a href="https://www.worldwildlife.org/" target="_blank">
        <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1518791841217-8f162f1e1131" height="200" alt="Wildlife">
    </a>
</article>

<article id="water">
    <h2>4. Water Conservation</h2>
    <p>
        Freshwater is essential for human survival, agriculture, and industry.
        Overuse and pollution of water resources are causing severe shortages.
        Conserving water ensures availability for future generations.
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>Fix leaking taps and pipelines</li>
        <li>Practice rainwater harvesting</li>
        <li>Avoid water wastage in daily activities</li>
        <li>Reuse wastewater for gardening</li>
    </ul>
    <a href="https://www.unesco.org/en/water-security" target="_blank">
        <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1509395176047-4a66953fd231" height="200" alt="Water Conservation">
    </a>
</article>

<article id="trees">
    <h2>5. Tree Plantation</h2>
    <p>
        Trees absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen, helping reduce global warming.
        They prevent soil erosion, provide shelter to wildlife,
        and improve air quality.
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>Organize tree plantation drives</li>
        <li>Protect existing forests</li>
        <li>Encourage community participation</li>
        <li>Promote urban green spaces</li>
    </ul>
    <a href="https://www.arborday.org/" target="_blank">
        <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1506744038136-46273834b3fb" height="200" alt="Tree Plantation">
    </a>
</article>
